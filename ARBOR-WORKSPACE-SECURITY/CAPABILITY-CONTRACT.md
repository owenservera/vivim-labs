# Proposed capability contract

Extend existing Host grant vocabulary and ledger. No parallel grant authority. Authenticated principal, device, workspace, resource identity, operation, program identity/version/digest, session, permitted data classes and destinations, expiry, revocation epoch, delegation prohibition, budget, approval policy and relevant resource revision are bound to effective authorization. Effective permissions are the intersection of owner, device, program, resource and session constraints, minus explicit denies. Recheck immediately at the real effect/read boundary, including after long-running preparation. Grant issuance and widening require owner authority; model text cannot authorize.

Example: weekly research may read public web and append to its authorized research Wiki section; no sensitive context export, email reads, program installation or delegation. Standing grants permit routine operations with no repeated prompts. Irreversible actions require appropriate exact approval. Canonical digest binds actual arguments, destination and expected resource revision. Use existing dispatcher, journal and receipts; never claim exactly-once semantics for an external service that cannot provide them.
