# Bounded V1 candidate — R001
Status: proposal for founder ratification, not accepted release.
Promise: My machine, my data, my AI. On my Windows PC and paired phone, I can talk to my existing AI, find and use my own information, and ask VIVIM to perform understandable local actions, with continuous context and control.

Candidate primary persona: individual existing AI subscriber with a Windows PC and Android phone; not assumed developer-only, pending D01. First Contact is a quiet single composer, not a dashboard. Setup can be invoked as needed; no mandatory history backfill before first usefulness. One GPUI surface and authenticated phone viewport; Host owns effects, receipts and persistence.

## Required proposed V1 outcomes
- J01 install/start, choose local workspace, resume safely without donor checkout.
- J02 pair phone, revoke/reconnect and see live bidirectional same-surface state.
- J03 connect real user-owned ChatGPT account, start/select thread, send and receive; continue same thread from either device.
- J04 locate and attach a selected local file to an intended conversation; explicit outbound disclosure.
- J05 understand familiar natural language locally: open a registered target, search authorized workspace, show supported command, preview or clarify; execute only through Host.
- J06 see live AutoWiki/source evidence while typing; inspect a source, correct a scoped user-authored fact or note, persist and reopen.
- J07 inspect workspace and meaningful local state; see capabilities and limitations rather than fictitious coverage.
- J08 recover from offline/failed/uncertain operations, restart and revoke; no duplicate effects or lost durable data.

## Proposed exclusions (not deletions)
Full arbitrary web/app coverage, arbitrary autonomous agent orchestration, unrestricted shell for everyday users, every historical capability, APK, additional operating systems, model-training/2035 intelligence breakthroughs, monetization/team features, full app builder, complete historical sync and multi-provider/agent orchestration as mandatory launch blockers unless owner explicitly includes them. Additional providers and social adapters may be implemented on demand under existing Host architecture without automatically expanding V1. No fabricated feature availability. Preserve deeper capabilities behind truthful progressive disclosure. Engineering independence is a dependency/workstream, not a consumer-facing feature claim.

## Boundary rule
Every MUST must trace to a specific observable journey and actual device acceptance. Source-implemented, tested, deployed and human-verified are separate states. Proposed narrower V1 does not supersede B01–B12 until owner ratifies differences. Do not ship or tag on this document alone. See decisions for persona, provider breadth, history, workspace lifecycle, commands and distribution.
