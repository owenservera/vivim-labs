# VIVIM V1 — Onboarding experience R002
2026-11-25 | Founder-confirmed direction; detailed design proposed.

## Canonical owner correction
Installation/workspace and preferred AI connection are first. After human sign-in and scoped context-acquisition authorization, start pulling user context from the chosen AI immediately. Phone pairing, chat attachments, commands, Wiki, workspace access and recovery are supporting capabilities, not sequential journey stages. Onboarding introduces VIVIM by naturally demonstrating its value.

## Proposed experience
1. **Arrive:** Install and open native GPUI. Establish owner-selected local workspace outside source. Explain personal ownership.
2. **Connect:** “Which AI knows you best?” Select preferred provider, human sign-in, verify account identity; explain and obtain scoped acquisition consent. No fake provider support.
3. **Discover:** Start actual authorized acquisition immediately. Show real evidence as it arrives: conversations, projects, decisions, preferences and history. Prioritize meaningful context; expose actual coverage, errors, pause/resume. No complete-history claim without proof.
4. **Recognize:** Reveal personally meaningful information from the user's actual context. Make inferred versus verified facts clear. Inspect sources, correct or exclude information.
5. **Experience ownership:** Demonstrate local knowledge, correction and durable persistence; no second database or effect owner.
6. **Experience usefulness:** Invite a meaningful action using actual context, such as continuing a project or asking what the user was working on. Reveal existing capabilities contextually rather than forcing a tour.
7. **Continue:** Setup naturally becomes the normal GPUI composer and workspace. Acquisition may continue within authorized, observable jobs; never block first usefulness on bulk import.

These are experience beats, not mandatory separate screens. Proposed evolving canvas: preferred AI connection area becomes live personal knowledge reveal, then the usable composer. Quiet First Contact remains the post-setup default. Phone pairing appears when relevant, not before personal value.

## Boundaries
Owner confirmed opening order, preferred AI, immediate context acquisition and value discovery through setup. Exact first reveal, provider launch set, breadth of history, sync cadence, and audience remain undecided. Keep account, acquisition, retention and outbound disclosure scopes distinct. Reuse Host/Studio/Personal World/AutoWiki; preserve source provenance, consent, revocation, deduplication and correction. No passwords or private histories in Git. No new renderer. [Existing ChatGPT-first design](../../personal-knowledge-lifecycle/CHATGPT-FIRST-USER-WORKFLOW.md).

## Acceptance
Real fresh install, preferred AI selection, verified sign-in, scoped acquisition, first real evidence and personally recognizable value within setup. Measure time to first evidence and first useful value; thresholds TBD. Demonstrate correction, next useful action, interruption and revocation. No device acceptance claimed.
