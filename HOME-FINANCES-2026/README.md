## Dataset
- transactions.csv: 368 transactions, operation date, page provenance, normalized merchant/concept, provisional category, signed EUR amount, computed balance, stable sequential ID and merchant code.
- monthly.csv: monthly cash flow, ATM withdrawals, groceries, benefits, transfers, closing and lowest observed balance.
- categories.csv: signed net flows by category (negative=outflow), counts.
- reconciliation.csv: all eight page totals independently match printed closing balances to €0.00.
- This report: findings, proposed budget and verification actions.
November is partial. Cash withdrawals are account outflows, not confirmed final spending. Classification is provisional; not a comprehensive household asset/debt inventory. Transfers are receipts, not necessarily earned income.
