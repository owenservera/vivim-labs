# Championship concepts P022–P032 — eleven new architectural hypotheses

Status: CONCEPT / UNIMPLEMENTED / UNQUALIFIED. Extends the first 21 conceptual entrants to 32 of the required 64, under [Constitution R003](../CONSTITUTION-R003.md). IDs refer to the new constitutional design catalogue, not historical R002 roster slots (which were assigned to different combinations). No claim of originality relative to existing research. These are alternative mechanisms for maintaining executable mathematical history, generating native code and proving promotion; traditional blocks, linear chains, coins and voting are optional abstractions, not design constraints.

Common obligation: independently generate each entrant's hardware-native seed (add/subtract/multiply/divide/negate/remainder/comparison), stress-test it in R1, validate complete machine-to-Rust bootstrap, use qualified native methods for the entrant's actual computational state, and evolve its own generator and adversaries. Every evolutionary advancement requires independently measured improvement in hardware-native promotion ability under equivalent budget, with correctness/ABI/recovery as hard requirements. The seed directions below are design hypotheses, not shared source or proof. Stay in standard calculator scope. Independent oracle, minimal recovery anchor and sealed holdouts remain outside candidate authority.

## P022 — Reversible Chronicle
State is a sequence of reversible transitions or explicit inverse receipts for information-losing steps. Admission must demonstrate forward execution and reconstruction of the preceding qualified state; irreversibility is represented, not wished away. Seed direction: native add/sub inverse pairs, checked multiply and quotient/remainder with retained operands and status. The generator evolves paired forward/inverse programs; the adversary searches for loss of information, overflow and faulty rollback. Promotion: more qualified native transitions under equal budget with preserved round-trip and recovery evidence. This does not assert ordinary division or all arithmetic is inherently reversible.

## P023 — Obligation Lattice
Replace linear block ordering with a partially ordered set of typed obligations (arithmetic law, ABI, artifact, dependency, recovery, performance). A capability becomes usable only when its required obligations are independently discharged; independent progress can merge without declaring unrelated work verified. Seed direction: independent carry/borrow and signedness proofs over limb representations. The generator searches for implementations that discharge more obligations with reusable evidence; adversary attacks missing obligations, invalid joins, stale proof dependencies. Advancement: demonstrably higher qualified native promotion yield, not merely more obligation entries. Canonical frontier and replayable history still bind decisions.

## P024 — Executable Genome
Store a versioned instruction-generation grammar as the evolving state, not just a library of finished routines. A candidate genome describes legal operand mappings, instruction templates, register constraints and recombination rules; its phenotype is assembled machine code. Seed direction: independently generate arithmetic from a small ISA-aware grammar. Its adversary mutates genes, explores ABI and arithmetic failures, and learns invalid combinations. Advancement: the new grammar yields more independently qualified native capabilities, or same yield at lower measured cost, including unseen calculator tasks. No candidate changes the external qualification policy.

## P025 — Metabolic Ledger
Treat computational resources as a conserved research budget, not a currency. Native generation, proof mining, replay and maintenance spend measured resources; evidence reuse frees budget for genuinely new promotion. The ledger's own accounting uses its qualified arithmetic. Seed direction: low-allocation native integer primitives and counters. A self-designed scheduler invests in methods with higher measured verified promotion yield; adversary targets fake resource savings, duplicate evidence and metric gaming. Advancement: higher qualified native promotion per equal compute budget, or equivalent yield with significantly lower resources; never reduce safety coverage to save cost.

## P026 — Cellular Proof Fabric
A candidate is a fabric of small local machine-code fragments with typed inputs/outputs, explicit clobbers and composition contracts. Neighboring fragments propose local rewrites; a whole-program checker verifies global effects and ABI. Seed direction: independently construct limb arithmetic cells and carry/borrow propagation paths. The adversary introduces boundary incompatibilities and overlapping register/resource use. Advancement: more qualified native compositions or reduced composition/promotion cost on unseen calculator expressions. Local correctness alone cannot establish global correctness; global replay and independent anchor remain mandatory.

## P027 — Shadow Twin
Maintain two independently generated native implementation lineages for the SAME mathematical semantics; one serves current calculations while the other shadows and challenges. Divergence becomes a counterexample, not majority truth. Seed direction: one limbwise implementation and one independently generated alternative representation/algorithm with a tested adapter. The adversary searches correlated defects and creates discriminating workpieces; a third independent oracle adjudicates. Advancement: faster or broader qualified native replacement and detection of previously unseen faults under equal budget. Both twins agreeing is not sufficient evidence; no shared faulty oracle.

## P028 — Negative-Space Atlas
Represent the unverified parts of the input and state space explicitly as a frontier: verified finite cases or certified regions, refuted regions, and unknown regions. No blanket claim of correctness from a passing sample. Seed direction: native arithmetic with explicit domain partitioning (sign, carry, overflow, divisor zero and limb-width boundaries). The generator targets gaps or worst regions, adversary hunts holes at boundaries and attempts invalid extrapolation. Advancement: broader independently justified native capability coverage or higher verified promotion yield within budget. Unknown outside the qualified domain remains unavailable or explicitly deferred; proof of a region requires an appropriate independent certificate, not sampling alone.

## P029 — Transplant Architecture
The chain evolves by exchanging complete native computational organs across a fixed, tested interface. A new organ is evaluated in a quarantined clone of the actual chain, then in Rust consumers; an atomic activation maps the old contract to the new qualified backend. Seed direction: separately generated native modules with explicit ABI and representation manifests. The adversary tests aliasing, state migration, calling conventions, stale bindings and rollback, including fault injection after activation. Advancement: demonstrably better promotion/transplant success or cost on previously unseen native replacements, while preserving actual chain execution and recovery. A matching signature is not an adequate transplant proof.

## P030 — Reconstruction Ledger
A generation is valid only when its executable artifacts, evidence and dependency history can be reconstructed from independently stored content-addressed components. The objective is to survive corruption or loss of portions of the research state, not trust a single serialized chain. Seed direction: native checked arithmetic plus verified representation/serialization boundaries; use conventional hashing/redundancy infrastructure unless separately qualified. The adversary deletes, truncates and corrupts candidate artifacts and receipts; reconstruction replays exact bytes and verifies provenance against trusted commitments. Advancement: improved qualified native promotion/recovery success under equal budget; redundancy overhead must be measured. Reconstruction does not itself prove mathematical correctness.

## P031 — Constitutional Succession
The chain treats its implementation generator and verifier as replaceable institutions under a minimal immutable independent constitution. A successor runs in shadow against predecessor, historical failures and hidden evaluation; a separate anchor must approve the change. Seed direction: native arithmetic for actual transition and promotion accounting, with a minimal independently controlled checker boundary. The adversary proposes weakenings, collusion-like witness substitutions and self-approval loopholes. Advancement: successor promotes more correct native candidates under the same resources without weakening independent qualification. The anchor and owner-approved rules are not subject to autonomous override. Unlike the earlier self-designing verifier, the central experiment is governed migration of the entire promotion authority.

## P032 — Instruction Witness
Represent each promotion as machine instructions plus an executable-semantics witness. Concrete traces, instruction-level state transitions and, where feasible, independently checked equivalence certificates connect inputs, outputs, flags, registers and memory effects. Seed direction: independently generate native arithmetic with explicit flag/carry semantics and exact ABI clobber contracts. The adversary searches undefined assumptions, flag dependence, wrong opcodes, unintended memory writes and discrepancies between interpreter/model and hardware. Advancement: higher yield of independently qualified native implementations, or equivalent yield with less verification cost, on unseen calculator operations. Concrete traces establish particular executions only; universal claims require adequate independent proof. The machine model itself must be challenged.

## Research priorities and boundaries
P024, P026 and P032 are complementary hypotheses for generating, composing and qualifying actual native instruction sequences; P023 and P031 are complementary governance hypotheses. P022 and P029 focus on reversible change and bootstrap; P027 and P028 on epistemic confidence; P025 on measured promotion efficiency; P030 on durable reconstruction. These are subjective prioritization hypotheses, not results. Next extend the conceptual roster to 64, then freeze independent entrant manifests and qualification before implementation. All eleven require their own complete D01–D16 genesis deliverables before R1; none is tested or admitted by this document.
