# 02 — Twelve wow moments
Twelve **imagined product scenes**, not actual comparisons, UI screenshots or planned implementation tasks. These are storyboards to evoke a feeling.

## 01 · The name that disappeared
> “Where did the old source memory engine go?”

**The magic:** A luminous thread crosses two renamed folders, a split module and a Rust boundary. The result states which links are blob facts, symbol matches, or only possible successors.

**The truth:** It distinguishes survival of source, API, behavior and runtime instead of flattening them into one green check.

## 02 · Three versions, one heartbeat
> “Show me this project at three revisions.”

**The magic:** Three translucent layers stack in the same space. A draggable rail reveals what was gained, lost, weakened and left uncertain at every transition.

**The truth:** Same-repository pins never collapse into the moving branch name.

## 03 · The quiet empty room
> “Compare these repositories.”

**The magic:** Before consent, there is only an elegant empty stage: source selectors, explanation of access, precise scope, and the promise that nothing has been scanned yet.

**The truth:** Capability readiness is not mistaken for a completed comparison.

## 04 · Show me the exact scar
> “Why is this behavior marked changed?”

**The magic:** The view dives from the capability to a specific typed contract, a changed source range and the test that no longer proves the old invariant.

**The truth:** Every causal claim earns its link; correlation is labeled correlation.

## 05 · An alternate tomorrow
> “What if we had kept the old graph query engine?”

**The magic:** Two ghostly ‘worldlines’ appear: observed history and a hypothetical design. The imagined branch offers questions and trade-offs, never fabricated performance numbers.

**The truth:** Hypotheticals are clearly watermarked and require explicit simulation if ever executed.

## 06 · The lost test constellation
> “Which promises lost their proofs?”

**The magic:** Capabilities sparkle, but several have dim halos. Those halos mean missing or unexecuted acceptance evidence, not necessarily broken behavior.

**The truth:** Absent test ≠ failed product; coverage is evidence-scoped.

## 07 · The five-minute handoff
> “Explain this subsystem to a new contributor.”

**The magic:** An evolving story introduces the interface, ownership, upstream dependencies, decisions and known mysteries. Each chapter can open its supporting sources.

**The truth:** A guide knows where it is uncertain and invites inspection.

## 08 · The migration x-ray
> “Did we really migrate the feature?”

**The magic:** A transparent anatomy separates copied bytes, reachable handlers, typed contracts, permissions, tests, receipts and device evidence.

**The truth:** No single source match masquerades as runtime parity.

## 09 · A conversation with a commit
> “What did this change intend to fix?”

**The magic:** The system stages an evidence-backed dialogue between the commit, its tests, the linked issue and its current successor. Claims are tagged observed or inferred.

**The truth:** Commit messages are witnesses, not authoritative truth.

## 10 · The architecture orchestra
> “What would this refactor disturb?”

**The magic:** Interfaces animate like instruments in a score. One change reverberates through contracts and tests. The system proposes an inspection itinerary, not an automatic edit.

**The truth:** Impact is a bounded projection, not predictive certainty.

## 11 · The beautiful contradiction
> “Why do these two teams both claim ownership?”

**The magic:** Two source-grounded histories converge in a bright collision. Instead of arbitrarily choosing a winner, HQ displays the disputed contract and decision needed.

**The truth:** An unresolved ambiguity is a first-class answer.

## 12 · A future-proof invitation
> “Can HQ do a comparison later?”

**The magic:** The agent answers yes, shows the native capability, explains required permissions and supported inputs, and leaves the system untouched.

**The truth:** Availability and authorization are separate; there is no silent background run.

## The underlying feeling
The delightful moment is not that an AI offers a confident answer. It is that an AI makes a huge, complicated history **feel navigable without hiding any of the uncertainty**. The visual wow is downstream of earned trust.
