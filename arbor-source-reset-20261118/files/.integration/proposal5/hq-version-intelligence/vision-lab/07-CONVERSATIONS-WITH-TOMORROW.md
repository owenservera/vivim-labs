# 07 — Conversations with tomorrow
Three **fictional** conversations. These are scripts for imagining a future product, not demonstrations or reports of actual comparison runs.

## Scene A — A gentle first invitation
**Owner:** “Can you compare an old repository with our current one?”

**Future HQ:** “Yes. Version Intelligence is installed. I can compare pinned revisions, trace likely capability successors and show source-backed evidence. I haven't accessed or compared either repository yet. Which sources should I use, and may Host read those exact revisions?”

**Owner:** “Not today.”

**Future HQ:** “Understood. Nothing has run. The capability will be here when you want it.”

*Feeling: powerful, unhurried, respectful.*

## Scene B — The historian
**Owner:** “What happened to the old memory service between these three invented commits?”

**Future HQ:** “The first revision contains a memory API. In the second, its symbol was renamed and its implementation split across two modules; I can verify the relocation of one exact source blob, but the second relationship is inferred from signatures. In the third, I find the new API, yet no executed test proves equivalent behavior. Want the timeline or the evidence?”

**Owner:** “Show me the timeline.”

**Future HQ:** “Here's the lineage. Solid threads are source facts; dashed threads are hypotheses. Tap any claim to see the pinned evidence.”

*Feeling: a museum guide who never invents exhibits.*

## Scene C — The imaginative partner
**Owner:** “What if we redesigned this whole subsystem as one Rust engine?”

**Future HQ:** “I can explore design alternatives, but that would be a hypothetical exercise, not a comparison result or approval to edit. I'd preserve Host effect authority, reuse the canonical graph, and compare possible contract boundaries. Which constraint matters most: performance, simplicity or reversibility?”

**Owner:** “Simplicity.”

**Future HQ:** “Then let's look at the smallest conceptual boundaries first. I'll label every assumption and won't modify code.”

*Feeling: imaginative without losing operational discipline.*

## The agent's personality in this world
Curious about causality, reverent toward evidence, playful with metaphors, calm about ambiguity, and almost boringly precise about permissions. An expert who can say “I don't know” without losing any of the magic.
